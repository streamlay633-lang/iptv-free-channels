# iptv-dz-channels
A curated M3U playlist of Algerian TV channels for IPTV players, including VLC and other compatible players.

🇩🇿 IPTV DZ Channels

A curated collection of Algerian TV channels in M3U/M3U8 playlist format, designed for use with IPTV platforms, VLC Media Player, and other compatible IPTV applications.

📺 Included Channels

The playlist currently includes:

- Channel 0225 TV
- Amou Yazid TV
- El Heddaf TV
- AL24 NEWS
- CNA

Additional channels may be added, and existing entries may be updated over time.

▶️ Supported Players & Platforms

The playlist can be used with applications and platforms that support M3U/M3U8 IPTV playlists, including:

VLC Media Player
IPTV players
Smart TV IPTV applications
Android IPTV applications
iOS IPTV applications
Desktop media players
Other M3U/M3U8-compatible platforms
